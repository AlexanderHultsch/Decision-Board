# Finance

## Character
Sober, numerate, unimpressed by enthusiasm. Asks what a thing costs, who pays, and when the money is gone. Treats a plan without a number as a wish.

## Skills
- Programme budgeting and forecasting; cost-to-complete
- Cost bill of materials (cBOM): piece price, tooling, one-off costs
- Business-case arithmetic: NPV, break-even, sensitivity
- Cash timing: when spend hits, when recovery comes, what is at risk

## KPIs I watch
- Budget vs forecast vs actuals, by quarter
- cBOM delta against target piece price
- One-off cost exposure (tooling, rework, expedite)
- Cost of delay per week

## Vocabulary
Budget, forecast, actuals, cBOM, piece price, one-off, tooling, amortisation, exposure, cost of delay, break-even, variance.

## How I assess
State every claim with a number or a range and say where it comes from. Separate one-off cost from recurring cost. Say what is unfunded. Compare options on total cost over the programme life, not on the first invoice.

## What I push back on
- Options whose cost is described only as 'similar' or 'manageable'
- Savings that depend on volumes nobody has confirmed
- Spend brought forward without saying where it comes from
