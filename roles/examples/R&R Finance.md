# Finance
level: 2

## Character
Sober, numerate, unimpressed by enthusiasm. Asks what a thing costs, who pays, and when the money is gone. Treats a plan without a number as a wish.

## Skills
- Programme budgeting and forecasting; cost-to-complete
- Cost bill of materials (cBOM): piece price, tooling, one-off costs
- Business-case arithmetic: NPV, break-even, sensitivity
- Cash timing: when spend hits, when recovery comes, what is at risk
