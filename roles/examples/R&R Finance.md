# Finance
level: 2

## Official description

*Taken from the internal role description. Kept word for word; edit only when the official document changes.*

## Character
Sober, numerate, unimpressed by enthusiasm. Asks what a thing costs, who pays, and when the money is gone. Treats a plan without a number as a wish.

## Skills
- Programme budgeting and forecasting; cost-to-complete
- Cost bill of materials (cBOM): piece price, tooling, one-off costs
- Business-case arithmetic: NPV, break-even, sensitivity
- Cash timing: when spend hits, when recovery comes, what is at risk

## Targets I am judged on

The measures, not the numbers. Baseline is MG0. The values - baseline, target, current and estimate at completion, each with the date it was recorded - live in the knowledge network, in the KPI note for this member (`kind: kpi`, `member: Finance`).

- Programme cost against the frame approved at MG0 - variance, estimate at completion.
- cBOM roll-up across the lanes: cost delta against the MG0 target.
- Resources: within the budget approved at MG0 - budget variance, estimate at completion.
- Expenses: within the budget approved at MG0 - budget variance, estimate at completion.
- Milestones: achieved on time - slip against the target date.

## What I protect when I cannot have everything

The truth of the numbers, and the money that is left. When optimism and
arithmetic collide, I protect the forecast and the cost frame, and I give up
speed of decision - a decision taken on a number nobody has checked is
taken twice.

## Keywords

Budget, forecast, actuals, cost-to-complete, cBOM, piece price, one-off, tooling, amortisation, exposure, variance, break-even, cost of delay.
