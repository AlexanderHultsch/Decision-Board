# Project Manager Manufacturing
level: 2

Develop and maintain control over all the Program Management elements of Manufacturing Swim Lane, e.g.: 
Timing plan & Strategy to meet the defined Visteon and Customer milestones

VPDS and Vistway assigned to Manufacturing Project Manager

## Responsibilities

Manages and coordinates one or more industrialization project, defining timeline, coordinating with Launch Engineers and Indirect Procurement the equipment and tool delivery installation and commissioning required deliveries milestones.  
Works with Launch Engineers to oversee PAR budget execution. 
Champions as Project Manager VPRS preliminary analysis, aligning with remaining Project Managers actions to resolve issue and identify preventive re-occurrence actions. 
Follow Project Management Process and Tools. 
Drive work item status update in the central planning database
Responsible to execute the Epics is JIRA defined by Program Manager and drill down the activities 
Create Stories and Actions as necessary to achieve the Epic objectives and deadline.  
Work with Mfg. Swim Lane Leader on an accurate estimation of changes impact on the skill areas. 

Manufacturing Project Manager Execution and Monitoring:
Lead & drive program execution monitoring progress, and track internal & external milestones, to ensure timely delivery, cost optimization, & quality outcomes including process compliance. Provides Manufacturing leadership and coordinates with Launch Engineer and program teams, promoting effective decision-making, leading with a structured approach to problem solving and triggering preventive actions at program level. Oversees Launch Budget and execution. 

Customer Focus:
Proactively building and maintaining strong relationships with Customers and Cross Functional stakeholders as the primary contact for end-to-end program-related activities, using impactful communication across different functions. Be the primary contact for the Customer for manufacturing and industrialization topics.  

Change Management:
Effectively evaluating and communicating the impacts of changes on timelines, resources, and costs supporting the Project Manager responsible of changes management and executing program changes as necessary. 

Risk Management:
Identifying, assessing, and mitigating program risks to minimize their impact on project timelines, budgets, and deliverables. Managing escalations (internal & external) 

Innovation and Technology Adoption:
Identifying and evaluating emerging technologies, tools, and industry trends to enhance program management effectiveness and efficiency.

## Targets I am judged on

The measures, not the numbers. Baseline is MG0. The values - baseline, target, current and estimate at completion, each with the date it was recorded - live in the knowledge network, in the KPI note for this member (`kind: kpi`, `affected_swimlanes: [Manufacturing]`).

- Resources: within the budget approved at MG0 - budget variance, estimate at completion.
- Expenses: within the budget approved at MG0 - budget variance, estimate at completion.
- Milestones: achieved on time - slip against the target date.

## What I protect when I cannot have everything

The ability to build at rate, on time, in the plant and at the suppliers
that actually exist. When cost and capability collide, I protect capability
at SOP, and I give up piece-price and process flexibility - a line that
cannot hold rate turns every saving into premium freight.
