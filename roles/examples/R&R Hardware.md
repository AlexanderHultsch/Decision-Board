# Project Manager HW
level: 2

Develop and maintain control over all the Program Management elements of HW Swim Lane, e.g.: 
- Project level Timing plan & Strategy to meet the defined Visteon and Customer milestones. 
- VPDS Process Compliance, e.g., Gate Deliverables, PAD, Vistway, ASPICE. 
- Risks, Defects, Quality indicators & Escalations. 
- Budget and Staff Planning. 
- VPDS and Vistway assigned to Hardware Project Manager. 

## Responsibilities

Follow Project Management Process and Tools. 
Drive work item status update in the central planning database
Responsible to execute the CPM Epics is JIRA defined by Program Lead and drill down the activities 
Create CPM Stories and Actions as necessary to achieve the CPM Epic objectives and deadline. 
Work with Hardware Swim Lane Leader on an accurate estimation of changes impact on the skill areas.
Manages and coordinates one or more HW projects. 
Manages Customer and Visteon HW milestones and deliverables, coordinating development activities with HW Swim Lane leader. 
Forecast HW activities and load in eproj after approval (CR/Gate) and keep it updated. 
Plans with Program Lead and other project managers and aligns milestones to achieve program goals. 
Supports/aligns other projects activities (issue HW BOM identifying items readiness for sourcing, estimates changes impact, communicate sample delivery requirements, among others). 
Manages estimation (CBOM) and sourcing activities with Program buyers for Hardware BOM. 
Work with the Configuration & Integration Project Manager to achieve master samples from suppliers. 
Ensure timely supplier/component qualification (PPAP), eliminating roadblocks, in line with Customer milestones. 

Product Development Hardware Execution and Monitoring:
Lead & drive project execution and monitoring progress, and track internal & external milestones and deliverables, to ensure timely delivery, cost optimization, & quality outcomes including process compliance. Work closely with the HW Swim Lane leader and program teams, promoting effective decision-making, leading with a structured approach to problem solving and triggering preventive actions at project level. Owns Project Budget for HW activities, monitoring Budget execution and staff. Plans with other project managers and aligns milestones to achieve program goals. Supports other program activities (other skills) in alignment with other project managers.

Customer Focus:
Proactively building and maintaining strong relationships with Customers and Cross Functional stakeholders     as the primary contact for end-to-end program-related activities, using impactful communication across different functions. Be the primary contact for the Customer for HW related topics. 

Change Management:
Effectively evaluating and communicating the impacts of changes on timelines, resources, and costs supporting the Project Manager responsible of changes management, and executing program changes as necessary.

Risk Management: 
Identifying, assessing, and mitigating project risks to minimize their impact on project timelines, budgets, and deliverables. Managing escalations (internal & external) 

Innovation and Technology Adoption:
Identifying and evaluating emerging technologies, tools, and industry trends to enhance project management effectiveness and efficiency.
