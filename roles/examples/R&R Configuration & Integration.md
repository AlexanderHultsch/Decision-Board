# Project Manager Configuration & Integration
level: 2

## Official description

*Taken from the internal role description. Kept word for word; edit only when the official document changes.*

Develop and maintain control over all the Program Management elements of Suppliers and Sample Swim Lane, e.g.: 
Timing plan & Strategy to meet the defined Visteon and Customer milestones 
Cascade Customer requirements for each milestone into release plan and SSOW 
VPDS Process Compliance, e.g., Gate Deliverables, PAD, Vistway, ASPICE. 
Risks, Defects, Quality indicators & Escalations. 
Budget and Staff Planning. 
VPDS and Vistway assigned to Configuration and Integration (C&I) Project Manager

## Responsibilities

Performs GBC releases in alignment with Customer / program milestones. 
Manages BOM, gathering inputs from other project managers including specification, drawings and status of readiness for procurement and release into GBC. 
Change Notices (CN) and Temporary Changes (TC) releases in GBC. 
IMDS requests in GBC and follow-up. 
Manage sample delivery activities, plan and define critical milestones in line with internal/external Customer requirements. Includes Customer samples, and DV/PV and homologation samples production. 
Includes coordinating with Procurement and MP&L to get the necessary components samples, 
manufacturing/assembly of samples and prototypes (production plant and/or ESC), and work with MP&L for the delivery to the required destination (Customer / internal or external lab). 
Works with Launch Engineers and MP&L to oversee Samples/Prototypes budget execution. 
Will support Program Lead on change management and other integration and coordination activities. 
Follow Project Management Process and Tools. 
Drive work item status update in the central planning database
Responsible to execute the Epics is JIRA defined by Program Manager and drill down the activities 
Create Stories and Actions as necessary to achieve the Epic objectives and deadline. 

Configuration & Integration Program Execution and Monitoring:
Track internal & external milestones, to ensure timely delivery, cost optimization, & quality outcomes including process compliance. Promotes effective decision-making, leading structured approach to problem solving and triggering preventive actions at project level. 

Customer Focus:
Proactively building and maintaining strong relationships with Customers and Cross Functional 
stakeholders as the primary contact for end-to-end program-related activities, using impactful 
communication across different functions. 

Change Management:
Executing program changes and scope by effectively evaluating and communicating the impacts of changes on timelines, resources, and costs with all Cross Functional stakeholders.  

Risk Management:
Identifying, assessing, and mitigating program risks to minimize their impact on project timelines, budgets,
and deliverables. Managing escalations (internal & external) 

Innovation and Technology Adoption:
Identifying and evaluating emerging technologies, tools, and industry trends to enhance program management effectiveness and efficiency.

## Targets I am judged on

The measures, not the numbers. Baseline is MG0. The values - baseline, target, current and estimate at completion, each with the date it was recorded - live in the knowledge network, in the KPI note for this member (`kind: kpi`, `affected_swimlanes: [Configuration & Integration]`).

- Resources: within the budget approved at MG0 - budget variance, estimate at completion.
- Expenses: within the budget approved at MG0 - budget variance, estimate at completion.
- Milestones: achieved on time - slip against the target date.

## What I protect when I cannot have everything

Release and BOM integrity, and the sample dates other lanes and the
customer depend on. When convenience and integrity collide, I protect a
correct, released, traceable configuration, and I give up the comfort of
late changes - a release that went out with a wrong BOM costs more to undo
than the change was worth.
